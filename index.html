01 / The manifesto

The right tools.
The right people.

Solving a problem is rarely just about knowing the answer. It is knowing what to examine, which tools to try, where to look next, and who already understands the terrain.

A useful lead can come from a manual, a forum, a local expert, an old friend, or an experiment that did not go to plan. The trick is knowing how to connect the pieces.

Life in Thailand

Life in Thailand / 01

My Thai Yellow Book and ID Card Journey

A long-term residency journey through Thailand’s yellow house book and Thai ID card processes, including paperwork, certification, witnesses, and the emotional weight of building a more permanent life abroad.

Life in Thailand / 02

Bangkok’s Hidden Gentleman’s Club – A Different Scene

A look inside a private cigar and whisky lounge where conversation, rare spirits, cigars, and the quieter realities of expat life take centre stage.

Life in Thailand / 03

This Is Why I Love Living in Thailand

A simple suki lunch opens into a conversation about retirement, relationships, cultural expectations, and the everyday adjustments involved in building a life together in Thailand.

Bangkok After Dark

Bangkok After Dark / 01

Bangkok After Dark: Exotic Massage Experience

A walkthrough of an exotic massage lounge exploring its rooms, services, presentation, and place within Bangkok’s overlapping wellness, hospitality, entertainment, and nightlife cultures.

Bangkok After Dark / 02

Hidden Bangkok Nights at Red Fern

A relaxed Friday-night exploration of Red Fern on Sukhumvit Soi 7/1, focused on friendships, local connections, new venues, and the social ecosystems that form around Bangkok streets.

Bangkok After Dark / 03

The Nightlife in Cha-Am

A walk through Cha-Am’s quieter nightlife district, contrasting its bars, beachside drinks, live music, and low-key atmosphere with Thailand’s larger nightlife destinations.
